01 / The main event

Come hungry.

Three James Beard–recognized kitchens. Three very different reasons to clear the evening.

The glamorous pick01
James Beard · 2026 semifinalists

The Town Company

Downtown · Hotel Kansas City

Think candlelit booths, a restored historic hotel, wood-fired cooking, and the kind of dinner that feels like an occasion before the first plate even lands. Elegant without being stiff.

For the family that wants to dress up a little and make a night of it.
Best fit: Sat Nov 21 or Sat Nov 28
Visit restaurant ↗Midwestern · $$$$
The adventurous pick02
James Beard · 3 chef nominations

The Antler Room

Longfellow · Kansas City

A warmly lit neighborhood room where every small plate is a little discovery. Global influence, daring combinations, excellent wine, and a menu that changes with the season.

For people who love to say, “Order one more thing for the table.”
Best fit: Sat Nov 21 or Sat Nov 28
Visit restaurant ↗Creative small plates · $$$
The unexpected pick03
James Beard · 2026 semifinalist

Of Course

Overland Park · Kansas

A confident mash-up of South Indian flavor and American ingredients. Surprising spices, shareable plates, serious cocktails, and a lively neighborhood energy.

For the group that wants something exciting, playful, and different.
Best fit: Sat Nov 21, Tue Nov 24, Sat Nov 28
Visit restaurant ↗Indian–American · $$$
02 / The nightcap

And one more round.

One destination in the Crossroads, one hidden gem in Shawnee. Both have James Beard recognition.

The stylish stop
James Beard · 2026 semifinalist

Fern Bar

Crossroads · Kansas City

A gorgeous old-bank-inspired space that makes a cocktail feel like a little ceremony. Thoughtful rum and agave drinks, low-key buzz, and plenty of atmosphere.

The “just one drink” that turns into a favorite memory.
Best fit: Sat Nov 21, Tue Nov 24, Sat Nov 28
Explore the bar ↗Craft cocktails
The secret-door stop
James Beard · 2023 national finalist

Drastic Measures

Downtown Shawnee · Kansas

There's a knock-at-the-door arrival, theatrical cocktails, and a room that feels like a hidden find. Part bar, part experience, all about the story you'll tell afterward.

A cocktail worth making a detour for.
Walk-ins only · 21+ · weekend groups max 6
