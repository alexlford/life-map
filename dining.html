ARCHIVE INTEGRITY

What counts?

A star is not the same as being in the Guide. Michelin Selected, Bib Gourmand, Green Stars, and one-to-three Michelin Stars are distinct recognitions. James Beard honors are tracked separately as winners, finalists, and semifinalists, naming the recipient and award year. This passport captures them all, but the starred-venues total includes only named, confirmed visits to restaurants awarded a star in the guide edition matching the recalled visit period. An approximate visit year does not establish whether the award came before or after the dinner.

Chef awards belong to the named individual, not automatically to the restaurant itself. Restaurant candidates are research leads, not extra restaurant visits. Michelin guide editions can take effect before their nominal year, so historical dates are cross-checked before crediting a star.
